# Support

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I add a contact?**
Tap **+** on the People tab or the Dashboard and choose **Quick Add** (just a name and how you know them), **Add with Details** (the full form), or **Scan Card**.

**What's the difference between Personal and Business contacts?**
Personal contacts focus on relationships, family, and interests. Business contacts include company, title, and professional details.

**Do I need to create an account?**
No. You can use Remember Who entirely offline with local storage. Cloud backup is optional.

**What happens after my first 14 days?**
Every new install gets full access to Pro features for 14 days — no card, nothing to cancel, nothing billed. Afterwards card scanning, voice notes, on-demand backups and calendar prep need Pro. Everything you created stays, automatic backups keep running, and you can always restore your own backups.

### Cloud & Backup

**How do I back up my data?**
Go to Settings → Account & Backup → Sign In, then tap **Back Up Now** and choose a backup password. After your first backup you can turn on weekly automatic backup. Backing up on demand is a Pro feature; automatic backups keep running without Pro, and restoring your own backups is always free.

**What's the backup password?**
It's separate from your login password. Your backup is encrypted on your phone with it before anything is uploaded, and it's never sent to us — so we can't read your backups. You'll need it to restore a backup or to view your contacts on the web portal.

**I lost my backup password.**
We can't recover it, and a backup can't be decrypted without it. Set a new backup password and make a new backup from a phone that still has your contacts. (Backing up on demand needs Pro after your first 14 days.)

**Is my data encrypted?**
Yes. Sensitive details (phone numbers, emails, notes, transcripts, action items) are encrypted on your device with AES-256; names, companies and places are kept searchable and protected by your phone's own encryption. Cloud backups are encrypted on your phone with your backup password before upload.

**Can I use the app on multiple devices?**
There's no live sync. You can restore a backup onto another phone (restoring adds anything missing; restoring the same backup twice adds nothing), and you can view your contacts read-only in any browser with the web portal.

**What is the web portal?**
Visit app.rememberwho.app, or tap Settings → Account & Backup → **Open web portal**, to view your contacts in a browser. It's read-only and shows your latest cloud backup, decrypted in your browser with your backup password. Sign in with the same email and password as the app.

### Features

**How do voice notes work?**
Tap the microphone icon on a contact to record a voice note. Transcription can run on-device (using Whisper) or via cloud (requires your own OpenAI API key). This is a Pro feature (included in your first 14 days).

**How do I scan a business card?**
Tap **+ → Scan Card**, or use the business card section when adding or editing a contact. Remember Who reads the name, title, company, phone, email, website and address on your phone (nothing is uploaded), and you review everything before saving. If the photo is sideways, tap **Rotate**, then **Re-read card** to scan it again — a re-read replaces what the last scan filled in and fills empty fields, but never changes a field you typed or edited. This is a Pro feature (included in your first 14 days).

**How does Calendar Prep work?**
Connect your Google, Outlook, or Apple calendar in Settings. The app matches meeting attendees to your contacts and shows preparation cards before upcoming meetings. This is a Pro feature (included in your first 14 days).

**Can I add locations to contacts?**
Yes. Add a location to a contact via the Map tab or the contact's detail page, or type an address and tap **Find on map** to drop a pin you can adjust before saving. You'll see them pinned on your map.

**Can I import contacts from my phone?**
Yes. When adding a new contact, tap "Import from Phone Contacts" to open the contact picker and select a contact.

**How do I scan a QR code?**
Open My Card (the QR button on the Dashboard) and tap **Scan Their Card**, or choose **Scan QR Code** when adding a contact. Name, phone, email, company, title, website, social and messaging links, and notes from a contact QR all carry into the form.

### Account & Data

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to Settings → Data & About → **Delete Account** (you need to be signed in). This deletes your account, your cloud backups and that account's contacts on your phone, so use **Export First** if you want a copy. See our [deletion page](delete-account) for details.

**How do I export my data?**
Go to Settings → Data & About → **Export Data** and save the file (for example to Files or Drive). **Import Data** brings it back. The export file isn't encrypted — keep it somewhere safe.

**I forgot my password**
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a link to reset your password.
